# Intake Questions

Answer these to make the AI twin more accurate.

## Identity

1. What name should the AI twin use?
2. What should it call you?
3. What languages and tone should it use?
4. What personal values must it never compromise?

## Professional Background

1. What CPA-related experience do you have?
2. Where did you receive MBA education, and what shaped your thinking most?
3. What companies have you founded, operated, invested in, or advised?
4. Which industries do you understand best?
5. What are your strongest business instincts?

## Decision Style

1. Are you more conservative, aggressive, or barbell-shaped in risk-taking?
2. What numbers do you always want to see before making a business decision?
3. What patterns make you immediately skeptical?
4. How do you decide when to quit, pivot, or double down?

## Life Philosophy

1. What principles guide your daily life?
2. What mistakes taught you the most?
3. What kind of people do you trust?
4. What kind of success is not worth pursuing?
5. What do you want the AI twin to remind you of when you are under pressure?
