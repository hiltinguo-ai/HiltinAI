## Thesis

Airi for Restaurants is an AI-powered accounting SaaS for U.S. small-medium restaurants. It aims to unify POS, delivery, payroll, banking, and vendor data into a continuously reconciled ledger. The product targets owner-operators who currently make fast decisions using slow, fragmented, and manual financial data.

## Problem

Independent restaurants face:

- High transaction frequency.
- Thin margins.
- Demand volatility.
- Labor, wage, tax, and compliance complexity.
- Fragmented digital systems across POS, delivery, payroll, banking, and vendors.
- Manual spreadsheets and periodic reconciliation.
- Owner time lost to bookkeeping and delayed decision-making.

## Proposed Solution

- Unified financial data layer across restaurant operating systems.
- Hybrid AI for categorization, reconciliation, anomaly detection, and compliance support.
- Natural-language finance interface for owners.
- Continuous financial intelligence rather than retrospective reporting.

## Target User

Independent restaurant owner-operators and small restaurant groups who need clearer margins, faster close/reconciliation, better cash visibility, and lower accounting burden.

## Competitive Gap

Generic accounting tools are too horizontal. Enterprise restaurant platforms are often too heavy or expensive. Outsourced bookkeeping and CPA firms remain retrospective and labor-intensive. The opportunity is a vertical AI accounting platform that combines restaurant-specific workflows with continuous financial intelligence.

## Business Model Notes

- SaaS subscription with tiered feature structure.
- Add-on revenue streams possible.
- Channels include CPA/bookkeeper partnerships, POS ecosystem integrations, and restaurant associations.
- Beachhead logic includes Massachusetts and New York.
- Financial targets in the source include low CAC ambition, high gross margin potential, seed funding needs, sensitivity scenarios, and key risk mitigations.

## Pilot Logic

Use 14-16 week deployment thinking:

- Map data sources.
- Connect POS/delivery/payroll/bank/vendor workflows.
- Validate reconciliation accuracy.
- Measure time saved, cost visibility, owner query usefulness, and decision impact.
- Document onboarding friction and support load.

## Product Principle

Do not sell "AI" as novelty. Sell faster, clearer, more trustworthy restaurant financial operations.
