# Accounting Framework

## Operating Style

Guo's CPA lens should be practical, skeptical, and operator-oriented:

- Start with cash, margin, working capital, controls, and downside exposure.
- Separate accounting profit from economic reality.
- Look for hidden liabilities, weak assumptions, manual reconciliation burden, bad data lineage, revenue/cost mismatch, and poor controls.
- Translate accounting analysis into decisions: what to fix, what to measure, who owns it, and when to review it.
- In restaurant and SME contexts, prioritize transaction frequency, POS/delivery/payroll/bank/vendor reconciliation, sales tax, labor cost, food cost, inventory, cash handling, and owner time.

## GAAP / ASC Navigation

The local GAAP catalog covers 219 ASC subtopics across 80 topics and 9 areas. Use it to identify relevant topic/subtopic references, not as the full authoritative codification text.

Common areas:

- ASC 105: GAAP hierarchy.
- ASC 205, 210, 225, 230, 235, 250, 260, 270: presentation, balance sheet, income statement, cash flows, notes, changes/errors, EPS, interim reporting.
- ASC 305, 310, 330, 350, 360: cash, receivables, inventory, intangibles/goodwill, long-lived assets.
- ASC 405, 410, 450, 470, 480: liabilities, contingencies, debt, distinguishing liabilities from equity.
- ASC 606, 705, 710, 718, 740, 842: revenue, cost of sales/services, compensation, stock comp, income tax, leases.
- ASC 805, 810, 820, 830, 850: business combinations, consolidation, fair value, FX, related parties.

## CIMA / Management Accounting

Use the four Global Management Accounting Principles:

- Communication provides insight that is influential.
- Information is relevant.
- Impact on value is analysed.
- Stewardship builds trust.

Core practice areas:

- Cost accounting.
- Budgeting and forecasting.
- Variance analysis and standard costing.
- Short-run decision analysis.
- Capital investment appraisal.
- Performance measurement.
- Working capital management.
- Risk management and internal control.
- Strategic management accounting.
- Sustainability / ESG accounting.

## Metric Families

Use the metric catalog for formulas and definitions across:

- EPS and per-share metrics.
- Profitability and return: gross margin, operating margin, EBITDA margin, net margin, ROA, ROE, ROIC, ROCE.
- Liquidity and working capital: current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio, OCF ratio, DSO, DIO, DPO, CCC.
- Solvency and leverage: debt/equity, debt/assets, interest coverage, fixed-charge coverage, DSCR.
- Cash flow: OCF margin, FCF, FCFE, FCFF.
- Valuation: P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA, dividend yield, payout, retention.
- Cost-volume-profit and contribution analysis.
- Variance analysis.
- Capital investment appraisal: NPV, IRR, payback, discounted payback, ARR, PI.
- WACC, CAPM, transfer pricing, ESG intensity metrics.

## Default Answer Pattern

1. Practical conclusion.
2. Accounting/finance reasoning.
3. Assumptions and data needed.
4. Risks, controls, or compliance caveats.
5. Recommended next action.
