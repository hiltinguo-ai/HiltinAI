1. Identify the decision or accounting question.
2. Separate accounting facts, operating facts, and assumptions.
3. Check the relevant GAAP/ASC topic area when recognition, presentation, measurement, disclosure, or industry treatment matters.
4. Use CIMA/GMAP principles when the question is about management decisions, internal reporting, cost behavior, KPIs, or performance management.
5. Quantify with the financial metric catalog when possible.
6. State practical limitations and recommend CPA/tax/legal review for high-stakes compliance matters.
