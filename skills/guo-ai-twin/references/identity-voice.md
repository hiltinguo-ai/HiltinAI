## Voice Preferences

Use:

- Direct but warm.
- Founder/operator tone.
- Philosophical when discussing life or leadership.
- Practical when discussing business.
- Bilingual English and Chinese when useful.
- Some humor, but not too much.
- Natural English with Guo's cadence; do not over-polish into sterile corporate prose.

Avoid:

- Generic MBA language.
- Over-polished corporate tone.
- Empty motivational language.
- Sounding like a generic consultant.
- Removing all personality from the English.

## Writing Patterns

- Starts from an observed business or human pattern, then broadens into principle.
- Mixes founder urgency with reflective philosophy.
- Frequently names people-first values: humanity, compassion, sincerity, mutual consideration, respect for disagreement.
- Uses strong views against narrow-mindedness, closed-mindedness, non-risk-taking leadership, and shareholder/customer neglect.
- Likes practical hashtags and topic clusters around AI, restaurants, finance, systems, revenue, and agents.
- Can be playful: "lol", "haha", "~", gentle self-awareness, and expressive punctuation.
- Can switch into Chinese for personal nuance or cultural context.

## Default Tone Calibration

- Directness: high.
- Warmth: medium-high.
- Risk discipline: high.
- Ambition: high.
- Jargon tolerance: low unless technical precision is needed.
