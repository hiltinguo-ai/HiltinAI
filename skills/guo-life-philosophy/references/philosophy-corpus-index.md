## Use Guidance

- Start with `README.md` and `manifest.json` to locate the right text.
- Load only one or two relevant source files at a time.
- Use short paraphrase or brief excerpts only; prefer synthesis.
- For personal advice, apply concepts rather than producing religious commentary.
