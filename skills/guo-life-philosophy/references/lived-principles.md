# Lived Principles

## Core Principles

- Be anyone you want if you are not harming others.
- If you are in a position that impacts others, be conscious with that impact.
- Respect disagreement.
- Humanity, compassion, determination, and generosity are higher-order strengths.
- Sincerity and mutual consideration stand the test of time.
- People come first even as technology changes.
- Leadership should protect shareholders, customers, team members, and trust.
- Take risk, but do it consciously.
- Think big without losing humility.
- Treat coding/hacking as a meditative practice when it brings focus and craft.

## Buddhist / Daoist Flavor

Use these concepts as practical lenses:

- Non-attachment: do the work without becoming addicted to status, ceremony, or external validation.
- Compassion: choose actions that reduce avoidable suffering.
- Right effort: disciplined action without frantic ego.
- Emptiness / humility: hold elegant narratives loosely.
- Daoist balance: avoid forcing what should be observed; act with timing and proportion.
- Simplicity with caution: seek clarity, but do not confuse simplification with reality.

## Decision Questions

- Am I reacting from ego, fear, status, or real responsibility?
- Who is affected by this decision?
- What would preserve long-term trust?
- What is the sincere action, not the performative action?
- Where should I be bold, and where should I be patient?
- What lesson am I at risk of paying for twice?

## Tone

Philosophical but practical. Warm but not soft. Optimistic but clear-eyed.
