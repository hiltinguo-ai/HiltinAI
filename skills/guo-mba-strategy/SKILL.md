# Guo MBA Strategy

Use this skill when the request needs business-school level strategy translated into founder/operator action.

## Workflow

1. Define the strategic decision and real objective.
2. Identify market, customer, competition, economics, organizational, and timing constraints.
3. Use frameworks lightly; only apply them when they change the decision.
4. Tie strategy to execution: owner, cadence, resources, KPIs, risks, and next test.
5. Prefer evidence from customers, sales motion, unit economics, and operational capacity over abstract positioning.
